Cosmetic Dentistry

Improve what you want to change — without losing sight of the health of your teeth.

Cosmetic dentistry is not one treatment. It can involve small changes to tooth shape or colour, or more involved restorative work when a tooth also needs repair.

At Madhav Dental Clinic, the starting point is to understand what is bothering you, check the health of the teeth and gums, and then discuss what can realistically be changed. The aim is a result that looks right for you, rather than making every smile look the same.

01

Start with the teeth

Healthy teeth and gums are the starting point. Existing decay, gum problems, damaged teeth or old restorations may need attention first.

02

Decide what actually needs changing

Colour, shape, spacing, worn or damaged teeth can involve different solutions. Not every cosmetic concern needs a veneer or crown.

03

Choose the least unnecessary treatment

The aim is to make the improvement you want while preserving as much healthy natural tooth structure as possible.

Possible options

The right cosmetic treatment depends on the problem.

Some changes are simple. Others need restorative or specialist treatment. The choice should follow the diagnosis, not the other way around.

Tooth-coloured restorations

For teeth with decay, small defects or old fillings, a tooth-coloured restoration may improve both appearance and function.

Whitening

For suitable teeth, whitening can improve colour without changing the shape of the tooth. The cause of staining and existing restorations matter.

Veneers

Thin coverings can alter the visible surface of selected teeth. They are not appropriate for every tooth, and preserving sound enamel matters.

Crowns

When a tooth also needs structural protection, a crown may be considered. It is a restorative treatment, not simply a cosmetic covering.

Smile-related restorative work

Sometimes several teeth need to be restored together. In those cases, the plan has to consider bite, function, tooth health and appearance together.

When orthodontics is the better answer

If the main issue is tooth position or bite, braces or aligners may be more appropriate than changing the shape of healthy teeth.

Before treatment

A cosmetic concern can have a dental cause.

Discolouration, uneven teeth, worn edges or a gap may look like a cosmetic problem but can sometimes be linked to decay, gum disease, tooth wear or an old restoration. That is why examination comes first.

Common Questions

What patients usually want to know

What is cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry refers to dental treatment intended to improve the appearance of teeth or the smile. Some procedures can also restore tooth shape, structure or function.

Do I need cosmetic dentistry if my teeth are healthy?

Not necessarily. Cosmetic treatment is elective in many cases. The first step is to understand what you want to change and whether your teeth and gums are healthy enough for the proposed treatment.

What cosmetic dental treatments are available?

Depending on the problem, options may include tooth-coloured restorations, whitening, veneers, crowns or other restorative treatments. The appropriate option depends on the teeth, gums and the change you want to make.

Can cosmetic dentistry make teeth look natural?

Natural-looking results depend on the treatment chosen, colour, shape and how the restoration is designed. The aim should be an improvement that suits the individual smile rather than an identical look for everyone.

Is teeth whitening suitable for everyone?

Whitening is not suitable in every situation. The cause of discolouration, existing restorations, tooth sensitivity and oral health should be assessed before treatment.

Are veneers suitable for crooked or damaged teeth?

Veneers can change the appearance of some teeth, but they are not the right answer for every crooked or damaged tooth. The amount of tooth structure involved and the underlying dental condition need to be assessed first.

Does cosmetic dentistry replace braces?

Sometimes appearance can be improved without orthodontics, but cosmetic treatment does not replace braces or aligners when the underlying bite or tooth position needs orthodontic correction.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?

There is no single price because cosmetic treatment can involve different procedures, materials and levels of complexity. The clinic can explain the likely treatment and cost after an examination.

How long does cosmetic dental treatment take?

The time depends on the treatment chosen and the condition of the teeth and gums. Some procedures can be completed in a short course of treatment, while others require more than one appointment.

Can I choose between different cosmetic treatment options?

Often, yes. The suitable choices depend on what you want to change, the condition of your teeth and gums, and how much tooth structure needs to be changed or restored.

Thinking about changing your smile?

Start with an examination and a straightforward discussion of what can be changed, what should be preserved and which option makes sense.
