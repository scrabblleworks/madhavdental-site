Dental Implants

Implant Treatment with an Expert Consultant Implantologist. A missing tooth may have more than one solution, and the right choice starts with a proper assessment.

A dental implant is a replacement tooth root placed in the jaw to support a crown or other restoration. It can be a useful way to replace a missing tooth, but it is not automatically the best choice for every patient.

At Madhav Dental Clinic, implant treatment is considered after looking at the tooth or gap, surrounding teeth, gums, available bone and the overall treatment plan.

01

First ask: can the tooth be saved?

If a natural tooth can be predictably retained, preserving it is considered before extraction and replacement.

02

Then assess the site

The gums, supporting bone, neighbouring teeth and bite all matter when deciding whether an implant is suitable.

03

Then choose the replacement

If an implant is appropriate, the implant and final restoration are planned as part of the same treatment strategy.

What treatment involves

An implant is a process, not just a single appointment.

The exact sequence varies from patient to patient. In a straightforward case, treatment involves placing the implant, allowing time for healing, and then fitting the replacement tooth.

Assessment

The missing tooth or teeth, gums, bone, bite and neighbouring teeth are assessed before treatment is planned.

Implant placement

The implant is placed into the jaw under local anaesthesia. The site then needs time to heal and integrate.

Healing

The healing period varies according to the case. Additional procedures may sometimes be needed before the final restoration.

Final tooth

Once the implant is ready, a crown or other suitable restoration is fitted and checked for function and appearance.

A careful decision

Implant, bridge or denture?

There is no single replacement that is right for everyone. The choice depends on the clinical situation as well as what works for the patient.

Implant + crown

Can replace a missing tooth without using neighbouring teeth as bridge supports, when the patient is suitable for implant treatment.

Bridge

Can replace a missing tooth using support from adjacent teeth. The condition of those teeth is an important part of the decision.

Removable denture

May be appropriate for one or several missing teeth, particularly where a removable option suits the clinical or practical situation.

Common Questions

What patients usually ask about implants

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a post placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth. Once the implant has healed and is suitable for restoration, a crown or other prosthesis can be attached to it.

Is a dental implant suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability depends on the condition of the mouth, gums and supporting bone, the tooth being replaced and the patient's general circumstances. An examination is needed before treatment is recommended.

Can an implant replace a single missing tooth?

Yes. A single implant can support a crown in suitable cases. Other replacement options, such as a bridge, may also be appropriate depending on the teeth around the gap.

Is an implant better than a bridge?

Neither is automatically better for everyone. An implant, bridge or denture may each be appropriate depending on the missing tooth, neighbouring teeth, bone, oral health, treatment time and your preferences.

Do I need a bone graft before an implant?

Not everyone does. Bone volume and quality are assessed during treatment planning. If there is not enough suitable bone, additional treatment may sometimes be recommended before or alongside implant placement.

Can I get an implant immediately after a tooth is removed?

Sometimes, but not in every case. The timing depends on the condition of the tooth and surrounding tissues, the available bone and the clinical assessment.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

The timing varies. Some cases can be completed more quickly, while others require healing time between stages. The schedule depends on the condition of the bone and gums, the type of restoration and whether additional treatment is needed.

Are dental implants painful?

Implant placement is performed with local anaesthesia, so the procedure itself should not be painful. Some soreness or swelling can occur afterwards and is managed according to the dentist's instructions.

How long do dental implants last?

Implants can have high long-term survival, but there is no lifetime guarantee. The implant and the replacement tooth can develop biological, technical or other complications, so regular care and follow-up matter.

What if my natural tooth can still be saved?

That should be discussed before extraction. If a natural tooth can be predictably restored, saving it may be an option. The decision should follow a proper diagnosis and discussion of the alternatives.

What are the alternatives to a dental implant?

Depending on the situation, alternatives can include a dental bridge or a removable partial denture. If a natural tooth can be predictably saved, treatment to preserve it may also be considered before extraction and replacement.

How much do dental implants cost?

There is no useful single price without assessing the case. Cost can depend on the number of implants, the restoration, the condition of the bone and gums and any additional treatment required. A treatment estimate can be discussed after examination.

Why does Madhav Dental Clinic involve a consultant implantologist?

Implant treatment can require specialist planning and surgical expertise. Where appropriate, the implant procedure is carried out in association with an experienced consultant implantologist, while the overall treatment plan is discussed with you based on your dental needs.
