Tooth Replacement

Replace a missing tooth with the option that fits your mouth, your needs and your treatment plan.

Losing a tooth is not just about appearance. It can affect chewing, the way your teeth meet and how comfortable your mouth feels. The right replacement depends on what is missing and the condition of the teeth and tissues around it.

At Madhav Dental Clinic, the starting point is a careful assessment rather than choosing an implant, bridge or denture in advance.

01

Single missing tooth

Depending on the case, replacement may involve an implant and crown, a bridge or another suitable restoration.

02

Several missing teeth

Options can include bridges, partial dentures or implant-supported restorations, depending on the position and number of missing teeth.

03

Many or all teeth missing

Full or implant-supported dentures and other restorative approaches may be considered after a broader assessment of the mouth.

The main options

There is more than one way to replace a missing tooth.

The best option is the one that makes sense for the individual mouth. Each approach has advantages, limitations and different maintenance needs.

Dental Implant + Crown

An implant is placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth. It can be considered when a suitable tooth replacement is needed without using neighbouring teeth as bridge supports.

Implant treatment at Madhav Dental Clinic is carried out in association with an experienced consultant implantologist.

Dental Bridge

A bridge replaces a missing tooth by using support from neighbouring teeth. Whether it is suitable depends on the condition and position of those teeth.

Partial or Complete Denture

A removable denture can replace several missing teeth or, in suitable cases, all the teeth. It may be a practical option when a fixed replacement is not appropriate or preferred.

What we look at

Choosing the right replacement starts with the right questions.

Before recommending a replacement, the condition of the mouth is assessed. The decision is not based on the missing tooth alone.

The missing tooth

Which tooth is missing, how long it has been missing and how it affects your bite and chewing.

Neighbouring teeth

The strength, position and health of the teeth that may be involved in a bridge or support the surrounding bite.

Gums and bone

The condition of the gums and available bone, particularly when an implant is being considered.

Your priorities

Function, appearance, maintenance, treatment time and what is practical for you also matter.

Common Questions

What patients usually want to know

What are the options for replacing a missing tooth?

Depending on the tooth, the surrounding teeth, gums and bone, options may include a dental bridge, removable partial denture or dental implant with a crown. The appropriate option is decided after an examination.

Is a dental implant the only way to replace a missing tooth?

No. A bridge or removable denture may be suitable in some situations. An implant is one option, not automatically the right choice for every patient.

Can a missing tooth affect the other teeth?

A missing tooth can change chewing and the way nearby teeth meet. The effect depends on which tooth is missing and the condition of the surrounding teeth and gums.

Can I replace several missing teeth?

Yes. Several missing teeth can be replaced with options such as a bridge, partial denture or implant-supported restoration, depending on the clinical situation.

How do I know which tooth replacement option is right for me?

The choice depends on the number and position of missing teeth, the health of neighbouring teeth and gums, available bone, your bite and your practical preferences. A clinical assessment is needed before recommending an option.

What affects the cost of replacing a missing tooth?

Cost depends on the number of teeth being replaced, the condition of the mouth, the type of restoration and whether additional treatment is needed. A meaningful estimate can be given after examination and treatment planning.

How long does tooth replacement take?

The time depends on whether the replacement is a bridge, denture or implant, and on the condition of the teeth, gums and supporting bone. Some options can be completed sooner than others.

How much does tooth replacement cost?

Cost depends on the number of teeth being replaced, the replacement method and any treatment needed before the replacement. An examination is needed to give a meaningful estimate.
